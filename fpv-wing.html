FPV Flying Wing

A modular 3D-printed fixed-wing FPV aircraft designed for reliability and easy field repair.

Model aviationiNavFlight controllers3D printingFPV electronics

Overview

Project scope

The goal was to create a flying wing with durability and operational reliability closer to a carbon-fiber FPV quadcopter. I adapted the Scimitar V2 design and used a Matek F405 Wing controller running iNav.

Challenge

Design problem

A printed airplane must balance stiffness, weight, repairability, electronics access, and aerodynamic performance.

Solution

Engineering approach

The airframe was divided into replaceable printed modules, allowing damaged sections to be remade quickly while keeping the flight-controller and FPV system easy to service.
