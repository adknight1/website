Summer 2025

PCC Streetcar Electric Motor Upgrade

Mechanical and electrical mounting systems for a battery-electric vintage trolley conversion.

SolidWorksSteel fabricationEV systemsHigh voltage packagingMachining

Overview

Project scope

At Vintage Trolley Inc., I designed and fabricated hardware for replacing outdated traction motors and resistor controls with modern brushless motors, inverters, contactors, and high-voltage components.

Challenge

Design problem

The modern system had to fit within the original trolley truck and use practical mounting locations while preserving service access and structural integrity.

Solution

Engineering approach

I created CAD assemblies and fabricated steel brackets that adapted new equipment to the existing truck, including inverter and contactor mounts and motor retention hardware.
