32-inch Gravel Bike

A custom chromoly gravel frame built around emerging 32-inch wheel components.

Frame geometryTIG weldingManual machiningMetal 3D printingPowder coating

Overview

Project scope

I developed the frame geometry from my current bikes and available pre-production references, then fabricated the frame from 0.035-inch chromoly tubing. The tubes were mitered on a milling machine, hand-fit in a frame jig, and TIG welded with stainless filler.

Challenge

Design problem

The rear triangle needed both large tire clearance and clearance for a 38-tooth chainring while supporting modern drivetrain and brake standards.

Solution

Engineering approach

Metal 3D-printed dropouts integrated a SRAM UDH derailleur mount and a flat-mount brake interface without additional welded or machined adapters.
