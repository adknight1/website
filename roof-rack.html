Winter 2024

Vannon V2 Roof Rack

A low-profile aluminum roof rack developed from prototype through production.

Product designCNC routingSheet metalPrototype fabricationDesign for manufacturing

Overview

Project scope

I supported the design and fabrication of a modular roof rack for Mercedes Sprinter vans while working at Vannon. The system was developed in CAD, prototyped on a large CNC router, formed, test-fit, and refined for repeatable production.

Challenge

Design problem

The rack needed a clean low-profile appearance, strong mounting points, efficient material use, and compatibility with different van layouts.

Solution

Engineering approach

The final design uses CNC-cut aluminum side rails, formed mounting tabs, and modular crossbars that simplify installation and allow accessories to be positioned as needed.
